# Meta template category definitions

version: 1
checked_on: 2026-09-18
source: https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/platform-pricing/

**This file is a working paraphrase, not verbatim policy text.** It records how
this project interprets Meta's published categories so that the interpretation
is versioned and reviewable. It is not Meta's internal classifier, and it does
not explain any individual decision. Re-check the source and bump `version`
when Meta changes its published guidance.

## UTILITY

A message that follows up on a specific transaction the recipient has already
entered into, or on a request the recipient has already made.

- U1. Refers to a specific, identifiable transaction, account or request — an
  order, invoice, payment, booking, appointment, shipment, ticket or case.
- U2. The recipient has an existing relationship that makes the message
  expected: they bought something, opened an account, or asked for support.
- U3. The content is confined to the facts of that event: status, amounts,
  dates, references, and what the recipient needs to do about it.
- U4. Any link or button leads to the transaction itself, not to a catalogue,
  storefront or promotion.

## MARKETING

Anything that promotes, re-engages or sells, including a message that is
otherwise transactional but carries promotional content alongside it.

- M1. Offers, discounts, coupons, sales, price drops or loyalty rewards.
- M2. Product announcements, new collections, catalogue or storefront links.
- M3. Upsell or cross-sell, including upgrades layered onto a service message.
- M4. Re-engagement: abandoned carts, "we miss you", win-back, renewals framed
  as an offer rather than a due notice.
- M5. Invitations, referrals or anything asking the recipient to bring others.
- M6. **Mixed content is marketing.** A genuine transactional update that also
  carries a promotional sentence or a promotional button is marketing.

## AUTHENTICATION

One-time passcodes and verification codes, and nothing else in the same
message.

## Interpretation notes

- The recorded dataset contains no case of a requested MARKETING template being
  recorded as UTILITY, so in practice the categories are not symmetric: the
  live question is only ever whether a utility-intent draft is downgraded.
- M6 is the rule most often implicated in this project's false-utility errors.
